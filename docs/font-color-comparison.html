Fonts and colors by place

Each sample uses that design's size, weight and color, drawn in its own font. Apple HIG is what Apple's Human Interface Guidelines recommend for a macOS app (SF shows only on a Mac or iPhone; elsewhere the system font). Fluent, Material and Apple values are from the published guidance as recalled, not checked in this session. Other columns keep the app's text, size and weight; add as many as you like with font URLs and your own colors. Use the column chips to show or hide a column, ◀ ▶ to move it, ✎ to edit and ✕ to remove an Other. Your layout and Other columns are kept in this browser.
